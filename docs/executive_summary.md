# Executive Summary
## Slow-Moving and Dead Stock Analysis System

### The Business Situation

The inventory analysis identified a significant amount of business capital tied up in products with weak or no recent sales.

Across the 20 products analysed, total inventory value was **₹1,14,39,250**. Of this, **₹81,09,400 (70.89%)** was associated with slow-moving and dead stock.

This represents a substantial opportunity to improve inventory management and reduce the amount of capital tied up in unsold products.

### Where Management Should Focus

**1. Computers — ₹34,32,000 at risk**

This is the largest category-level exposure. The Gaming Laptop 15-inch accounts for the entire amount and should be reviewed for pricing, promotional activity and future purchasing decisions.

**2. Office Equipment — ₹16,38,600 at risk**

All inventory value in this category is classified as slow-moving or dead stock. Further purchasing should be carefully reviewed against actual demand.

**3. Photography Equipment — ₹7,65,000 at risk**

The Entry DSLR Camera is the highest-value dead-stock product identified. Management should consider clearance pricing, bundling or liquidation.

### Recommended Priorities

| Priority | Recommended management action |
|---|---|
| High | Review the Gaming Laptop's pricing and future purchase quantities |
| High | Develop a clearance plan for the Entry DSLR Camera |
| High | Investigate demand and purchasing patterns in Office Equipment |
| Medium | Review other dead-stock products for discounts or supplier returns |
| Ongoing | Monitor new products and replenish fast-moving products based on demand |

### Expected Business Benefit

Implementing these recommendations could help the business make more informed purchasing decisions, reduce excess stock accumulation and improve the use of working capital.

The analysis identifies inventory exposure; it does not calculate guaranteed savings or confirmed financial losses. Actual outcomes would depend on customer demand, achievable selling prices and supplier arrangements.

### Conclusion

The analysis highlights a clear inventory management concern: **more than 70% of the inventory value is currently classified as slow-moving or dead stock**.

Management should prioritise the highest-value exposures, review purchasing decisions and establish regular inventory monitoring to prevent further accumulation of unsold products.
